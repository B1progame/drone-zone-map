# Aeris photography prompts

Use these prompts to commission or generate a cohesive original photo set. Keep all imagery photographic and editorial. Do not render text, logos, map pins, interface panels, grids, HUD marks, or watermarks into the image; the product UI adds those separately.

## Homepage hero — coast, 21:9

Wide cinematic aerial photograph for a premium European drone-flight planning product. A compact consumer quadcopter is clearly visible in the right third of the frame, flying above a rugged Atlantic coastline at blue hour just after sunset. Deep pine-green headland, dark teal water, a narrow band of warm apricot light at the horizon, realistic atmospheric haze, restrained natural color, crisp but not oversharpened. Leave the left half dark, quiet, and uncluttered for white headline and search controls. Documentary photography, believable scale, no people in foreground, no typography, no logo, no UI, no artificial neon, no fake flight HUD, no watermark. Horizontal 21:9 composition, designed for a dark overlay and responsive crop.

## Homepage hero — forest alternative, 21:9

Editorial landscape photograph from a small drone camera looking obliquely across a vast evergreen forest in the Bavarian Alps at first light. One small drone silhouette near the far right edge gives scale without becoming the subject. A thin river valley and distant blue-grey ridgeline create depth; soft mist catches the first pale gold light. Cool spruce, charcoal, muted jade, and a subtle warm horizon. Keep the left 55 percent low-detail and dark enough for legible white copy. Natural photographic realism, no dramatic fantasy clouds, no text, no map overlays, no logos, no UI, no watermark.

## Forecast page backdrop — restrained atmosphere, 16:9

Quiet, high-altitude aerial photograph above layered cloud and mountain terrain before sunrise. Broad soft cloud forms and a low distant ridge, cool slate-blue and deep spruce palette, gentle low-contrast light with a faint warm horizon. The composition should stay subdued behind translucent forecast panels; no single bright hotspot, no drone required, no text, no symbols, no map lines, no interface, no logo, no watermark. Photorealistic weather editorial image, horizontal 16:9.

Rich but natural greens, lifted shadows, restrained contrast, no people, no text, no logo, no UI, no grid, no overlay, no watermark. 4:5 editorial crop.

## Conditions feature card — mountain wind, 4:5

Photorealistic portrait landscape with a compact drone hovering at a safe distance above a broad alpine ridge. Fine cloud wisps drift visibly across the ridgeline to suggest wind; the drone is small and secondary, never close to people. Cool clean sky, muted stone, blue-grey distance, soft daylight, balanced highlights. Avoid storm spectacle and avoid implying an unsafe flight. No text, no HUD, no map graphics, no logo, no watermark. 4:5 crop.

## Closing call to action — coast, 2:1

Wide, calm editorial photograph of a drone pilot preparing a compact quadcopter at a coastal overlook shortly before sunset. Show the person only as a small, anonymous silhouette near the far-right edge, with open horizon and ocean across the center. Natural dusk colors: deep green-black foreground, dark teal sea, fine warm amber light. Keep the left half simple and dark for a short headline and button. Grounded, responsible pre-flight mood; no readable branding, no text, no UI, no flight path graphic, no watermark. Horizontal 2:1.
